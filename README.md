# IM-POWER: Integrated Modeling of Power Output of Floating Offshore Wind Farms

IM-POWER is a research project developing a numerical integrated model to predict the power generation performance of floating offshore wind farms during storm conditions and to anticipate their impact on power grids.

## Objectives
- Impact FOWT design by improving reliability under extreme weather.
- Support power grid planning and stability during storms.
- Provide protocols to estimate farm-level power output from high-fidelity turbine-level simulations.

## Methodology (high level)
- Multi-tier modeling approach combining environmental wave/wind propagation, farm layout representation, and turbine-level high-fidelity simulations.
- High-fidelity fluid–structure interaction using Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) within DualSPHysics, coupled with control and electromechanical models.
- Farm power output reconstructed from turbine simulations and analyzed for grid implications.

## Work Packages (summary)

- WP3: Development and implementation of new multiphysics tools (M3–M10).

## Current status
- Month 14 of 24. Development completed; site characterization and farm-level integration ongoing. Communication and dissemination active.

## Outcomes
- Software components for wind–blade interaction and control modeling within DualSPHysics.
- Procedures to estimate storm-time power output from floating wind farms.
- Open science practices: publications, data sharing, and training materials.
