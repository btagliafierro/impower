    Outcome

    This page collects the direct outputs of IM-POWER: results led from within the project, and results concerning floating platforms for wind energy.

    Headline result¶

    Nonlinear loading under severe sea states

    Structural demand rises sharply while power output does not

    For a semi-submersible platform carrying a 5 MW turbine under aligned wind and waves, resolving the nonlinear wave field leads to substantially larger surge drift and correspondingly higher average and peak loads in both the mooring system and the tower-base structural response, driven by higher-harmonic forcing and slow-drift excitation.

    Wave-state information is therefore relevant to power curve definition and farm-level resource assessment, which currently rely on a single generic curve. The coupled framework provides a basis for pursuing this, potentially through sea-state-dependent power matrices for floating offshore wind.

    High-fidelity assessment of nonlinear structural loading of a semi-submersible FOWT under severe sea states. RENEW 2026 - 7th International Conference on Renewable Energies Offshore. Accepted.

    Software and data outcome¶

    DualSPHysics v5.4: benchmarks, templates and open repository

    A validated implementation of the framework across four representative benchmark cases of wave energy devices (WEC), has been released together with a complete set of reproducible simulation templates. The accompanying repository contains simulation input files, XML metadata, geometries, wavemaker signals and execution scripts, so that every case can be reproduced from scratch. This is direcly applicable to the simulations of FOWTs. A new set of cases for these structures is being prepared.

    • Framework paper. Implementation and validation of DualSPHysics v5.4 for the simulation of four representative benchmarks of wave energy converters. Submitted to MethodsX.
    • Companion review. High-fidelity simulations for wave energy converters — a review of the contribution of the smoothed particle hydrodynamics method. Submitted to Energy Reports.
    • Open repository. Simulation templates and input data, CC BY 4.0 — 10.5281/zenodo.21793926
